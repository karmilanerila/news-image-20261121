# Sources — every fact on the front page, verified 21 November 2026

All reporting below is dated 21 November 2026 unless noted. Figures are as
reported by the source named. Where sources disagreed slightly (e.g. Peru

reported on the day, or gives a range.

## Lead stories

1. **Riyadh intercepts** — Saudi-led coalition intercepted and destroyed
   three Houthi ballistic missiles fired towards Riyadh early Saturday
   21 Nov; Civil Defence alert 01:53, announcement shortly after 02:00,
   two explosions heard, alert then lifted. A Houthi drone towards the
   south was also destroyed. Follows a Houthi strike on Thu 19 Nov that
   damaged the PIF tower (Riyadh's tallest). Sources: Arab News, Al
   Jazeera, Jerusalem Post, The Hindu, Channel NewsAsia, 21 Nov 2026.
2. **COP31 overtime, Antalya** — talks due to end Fri 20 Nov spilled into
   Saturday; ~200 nations deadlocked over a draft that drops all fossil-fuel
   mentions and a $120bn adaptation-finance target; third draft expected
   Saturday; EU warns the draft "risks turning this climate COP into a
   fossil summit"; 12 nations demand a fossil-fuel transition. Sources:
   Al Jazeera, Reuters, AP/Nikkei, Arab News, Dawn, 21 Nov 2026.
3. **BepiColombo at Mercury** — ESA/JAXA twin orbiters enter Mercury orbit
   Sat 21 Nov after an 8-year journey (launched Oct 2018; 9 flybys: 1 Earth,
   2 Venus, 6 Mercury); initial orbit 590 x 11,640 km; MPO and Mio separate
   9–10 Dec; science starts Apr 2027. Sources: ESA, Sky & Telescope,
   Scientific American, Wikipedia, 21 Nov 2026.
